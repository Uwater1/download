# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation is expected to be respectful, specific, and professional. Welcome different backgrounds and viewpoints. Focus criticism on the code or proposal, avoid personal attacks, and do not publish private information without consent.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and retaliation against someone who raises a concern.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately to the repository owner through the contact route in `SECURITY.md`. The maintainer may remove comments, restrict participation, or close contributions when needed to keep collaboration safe and productive. Reports will be handled discreetly and in good faith.
